# PW2VB01
## Specifications
* Working voltage: 10 to 30 V, 12 V or 24 V recommended.
* Working current: less than 2 mA.
* PWM duty cycle: 0 to 100%.
* PWM frequency: 50 Hz to 50 kHz.
* PWM level: 3.3 V or 5 V.
* Output voltage range: 0 to 5 V or 0 to 10 V.
* Output voltage error: less than 1%.
* Output voltage linearity error: less than 0.5%.
* Maximum drive current: 15 mA at 12 V or 20 mA at 24 V.
* Startup time: less than 2 ms.
* Operating temperature: -40 to 85 °C.
* Size: 29.5 × 16.5 × 6 mm.
* Weight: 2.2 g board only, 4.5 g with terminals.

## Pins
* `Vin`: power positive.
* `GND`: power negative.
* `PWM`: PWM signal positive.
* `GND`: PWM signal negative.
* `Vo`: output positive.
* `GND`: output negative.
